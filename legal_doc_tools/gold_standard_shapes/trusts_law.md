# Trusts Law Gold Standard Output Shapes

Internal QA only. Do not copy this wording into user answers.

## Shape 1: Purpose Trust / Secret Trust / Certainty Problem

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: classify every disposition. Separate express trust, gift, purpose trust, secret trust and residue.
- Part II: three certainties and formalities. Identify intention, subject matter, objects and any testamentary/formality issue.
- Part III: beneficiary principle and purpose trusts. Start with enforceability, then recognised exceptions, capriciousness and perpetuity.
- Part IV: secret trust. Identify fully secret or half-secret; communication, acceptance, timing and consequence of failure.
- Part V: resulting trust or residue consequence. Explain who receives failed property and why.
- Part VI: final drafting/remedy advice.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Starts with classification before remedy.
- Keeps certainty of objects separate from beneficiary principle.
- Treats secret-trust communication and acceptance separately.
- States the consequence of failure, not just that the trust is void.

### Common Fail Signals
- Turning every sympathetic purpose into a valid trust.
- Ignoring perpetuity or capriciousness.
- Treating fully secret and half-secret trusts as identical.
- Saying "resulting trust" without stating for whom.

## Shape 2: Breach, Tracing And Proprietary Remedies Problem

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: short advice and asset map.
- Part II: establish trustee/fiduciary status, duty and breach before tracing.
- Part III: personal claims: equitable compensation, account of profits, knowing receipt or dishonest assistance where relevant.
- Part IV: proprietary base and tracing route: original property, substitute asset, mixed fund, lowest intermediate balance and priority.
- Part V: defences, change of position, bona fide purchaser and limitation/laches if raised.
- Part VI: insolvency consequence and final remedy ranking.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Does not start tracing before proving breach/proprietary base.
- Separates personal and proprietary claims.
- Explains why proprietary relief matters in insolvency.
- Gives a ranked recovery route for each asset.
