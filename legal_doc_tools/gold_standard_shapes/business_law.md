# Business And Company Law Gold Standard Output Shapes

Internal QA only. Do not copy this wording into user answers.

## Shape 1: Director Conflict / Related-Party Transaction Problem

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: identify client and claim owner. State whether the company, shareholder, creditor or office-holder owns each route.
- Part II: board authority and articles. Analyse quorum, voting, conflict restrictions and actual/apparent authority.
- Part III: directors' duties. Apply duties separately to each director: proper purpose, success of company, care/skill, conflict, benefit and declaration.
- Part IV: approval route. Analyse substantial property transaction, shareholder approval, ratification limits, filings and valuation evidence.
- Part V: shareholder remedies. Separate derivative claim from unfair prejudice and personal claims.
- Part VI: remedies and practical steps: rescission, account of profits, indemnity, compensation, board/shareholder resolutions or litigation.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Does not say majority shareholding cures a conflict.
- Separates company loss from shareholder prejudice.
- Identifies who can sue and what remedy follows.
- Treats valuation evidence and procedural approval as distinct.

### Common Fail Signals
- Moralised director blame without statutory duty.
- Treating all directors as one block despite different knowledge.
- Using veil piercing where duties, agency, trust or statute is the real route.

## Shape 2: Insolvency And Office-Holder Remedies Problem

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: timeline and insolvency trigger.
- Part II: director-by-director knowledge and creditor-interest duty.
- Part III: wrongful trading, misfeasance, preferences, transactions at undervalue, unlawful distributions and security validity.
- Part IV: priority and practical recovery prospects.
- Part V: final office-holder strategy and likely contribution/remedy.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Uses dates and solvency evidence.
- Separates pre-insolvency corporate governance from office-holder claims.
- States remedy, defendant and evidential gap for each route.
