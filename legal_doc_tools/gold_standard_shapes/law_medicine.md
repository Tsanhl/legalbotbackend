# Law And Medicine Gold Standard Output Shapes

Internal QA only. Do not copy this wording into user answers.

## Shape 1: Course-Bound Medical Law Essay

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: thesis-led introduction. State whether reform or the current law is preferable and identify the statutory/common-law routes.
- Part II: first syllabus example in depth. Apply governing route before ethical critique.
- Part III: second syllabus example in depth. Repeat rule, application and critique.
- Part IV: optional third example only if it materially advances the thesis.
- Part V: conclusion. Synthesis and reform position.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Stays within the module syllabus unless the user expressly asks for no limit.
- Names governing legal route before ethics.
- Uses two or three examples deeply rather than surveying all medical law.
- Does not drift into negligence, mental health, deprivation of liberty, public health or US law unless expressly relevant.

### Common Fail Signals
- Autonomy rhetoric without statute/common-law test.
- Copying model introduction style rather than adapting the structure.
- Broad medical-law survey with no thesis.

## Shape 2: No-Syllabus-Limit Medical Law Essay

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: label broader scope and current-law caution.
- Part II: course-core legal route.
- Part III: wider English medical-law comparator.
- Part IV: reform/institutional competence.
- Part V: conclusion.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Clearly labels wider material.
- Verifies current-law status for reform-sensitive topics.
- Still keeps issue depth rather than becoming a list.
