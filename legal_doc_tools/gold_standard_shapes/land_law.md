# Land Law Gold Standard Output Shapes

Internal QA only. Do not copy this wording into user answers.

## Shape 1: Registered Land Priority Problem

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: short answer. Identify the purchaser, the register position and which rights may bind.
- Part II: land status and chronology. State registered land first, then list each transaction in date order.
- Part III: right-by-right analysis. For each asserted right, cover creation, formality, registrability/protection, priority and remedy.
- Part IV: actual occupation. Analyse occupation, discoverability, inquiry, consent and Schedule 3 paragraph 2 exceptions.
- Part V: overreaching. Separate overreaching from overriding interests; identify trustees, capital money and effect on the beneficial interest.
- Part VI: final outcome. State which rights bind the purchaser, which are lost or shifted to proceeds, and what immediate remedies follow.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Starts with registered/unregistered status.
- Separates easement, lease, option, beneficial interest, restriction/notice and covenant.
- Applies LRA 2002 section 27 and section 29 where relevant.
- Keeps actual occupation and overreaching analytically separate.
- Ends with practical remedy: declaration, injunction, damages, TOLATA order, rectification/alteration or priority advice.

### Common Fail Signals
- Treating actual occupation as an automatic trump.
- Applying unregistered-land priority rules to registered land.
- Collapsing an option and easement into one generic equitable interest.
- Forgetting that overreaching moves the beneficial interest into the sale proceeds.
- Stating fairness without property concept or remedy.

## Shape 2: Co-Ownership / Estoppel / TOLATA Problem

### Ideal Output Structure
- Part I: claimant objective and title position.
- Part II: acquisition of beneficial interest: resulting trust, common-intention constructive trust or proprietary estoppel.
- Part III: quantification of shares, keeping acquisition separate from size.
- Part IV: priority against third parties, including occupation and overreaching if raised.
- Part V: TOLATA or estoppel remedy, with proportionality.
- Part VI: practical conclusion and evidence gaps.

### Must-Have Quality Checks
- Distinguishes legal title from beneficial ownership.
- Separates acquisition from quantification.
- Applies assurance, reliance, detriment, unconscionability and proportionality separately in estoppel.
- States the remedy and why that remedy is proportionate.
